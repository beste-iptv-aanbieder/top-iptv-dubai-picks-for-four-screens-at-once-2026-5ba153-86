3. Expat / International Server Lines – Good Variety, Poor Sync

These services offer a wide range of international channels. The problem is high latency – the video lags behind the audio, especially during live events. You may notice that sound and picture do not match. The bitrate also changes often, making the stream look blurry one moment and sharp the next. Payment usually requires cryptocurrency or bank wire transfer, and customer support is only by email. While the channel list may look attractive, the poor synchronization ruins the experience on four screens.

4. VOD-Only Providers – Great Movies, Weak Live Streams

But if you want live streams (sports, news, events), these providers often fail. The live encoding is unstable, causing freezes and long loading times. As Washington Post Tech has reported, streaming performance varies greatly by provider. For a multi-screen household in Dubai that needs both live TV and on-demand, VOD-only is not enough.
